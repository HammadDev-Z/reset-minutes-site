The 10-Minute Daily Reset

Clear first, wipe second, floor last. Set a timer. When it rings, stop.

DoneStepTime budgetWhat to do
1. Put ten things back2 minTen items from floor, couch and table go where they live. No home? One basket.
2. Dishes2 minLoad the dishwasher, or stack dishes and leave them to soak.
3. Counters and stovetop2 minClear, then wipe the kitchen counters and the top of the stove.
4. Trash check1 minEmpty kitchen and bathroom bins if they are full.
5. Walkway floor pass2 minSweep or vacuum only the paths you walk every day.
6. Bathroom sink1 minWipe the sink and the counter around it.

The minimum day (about 5 minutes)

Rough day? Do these three and call the day done. You do not need to make up a missed day.

Dishes handled: in the dishwasher, or stacked and soaking
Trash out if it is full: one bin is enough
One clear surface: the counter or table where you will start tomorrow
Safety: Use one cleaning product at a time. Never mix cleaning products, especially bleach with vinegar, ammonia or other cleaners. Read the label, open a window or run the fan, and keep products in their original containers away from children and pets. In the US, Poison Control: 1-800-222-1222.
