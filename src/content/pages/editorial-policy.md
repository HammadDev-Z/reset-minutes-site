## Our commitments

1. **Purpose.** We write to help readers keep a small home clean in the time they have.
2. **Sources.** Safety statements come from official sources (for example poison-center, public-health and environmental-agency pages), and we link them next to the claim. Product instructions come from the product label or manufacturer.
3. **Times.** Times in our guides are **time budgets** (how long to spend), not measurements. No timing trials have been run yet. If we run trials later, we will describe the home, the method and the dates, and link the results.
4. **Experience.** We only describe experiences that a person actually had. The guides do not currently claim first-hand testing.
5. **AI.** AI helps with research notes, drafting and copy-editing. A person checks and approves every guide.
6. **Corrections.** We correct errors, and we note the date and what changed. We do not change a date without a real update.
7. **Updates.** We update a guide when a source changes or we find an error, and we change its date only for a real update.
8. **Independence.** If we earn money from a link or an ad, we say so near it. As of November 2026 we do not earn money from links or ads on this site.

## Not legal or medical advice

Our guides are general information. Follow product labels and the care instructions for your surfaces and appliances. Lease and deposit rules differ by place; check your own lease.
