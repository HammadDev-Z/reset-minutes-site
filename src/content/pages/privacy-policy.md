**Effective date: 25 November 2026.** This policy describes how Reset Minutes (www.resetminutes.com) handles information about its readers. It describes the site as it works today.

## What the site collects

- **Nothing from you directly.** The site has no accounts, comments, sign-up forms or newsletter.
- **Standard server logs.** Our hosting provider, Vercel, serves the site and may keep standard technical logs of requests, such as IP address, browser type and the pages requested. We do not use these logs to identify individual readers.
- **Analytics, but no ads.** As of the effective date the site uses Google Analytics 4 to count visits and understand which guides readers use: pages visited, approximate location (country and city), device and browser type, and how visitors arrive (for example, from Pinterest). We use these statistics to improve the guides and to measure the site's audience. The site shows no advertising, and we do not use analytics data to identify individual readers or for advertising.

## Cookies and similar technology

The site itself sets no cookies of its own. Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (such as `_ga`) in your browser so it can tell visits apart and count returning visitors; these are the only cookies the site causes to be set. Search works in your browser against a file on the site, and your search words are not sent to us or to anyone else. Apart from the Google Analytics tag, the site loads no third-party scripts, embedded videos or social widgets. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or use Google's opt-out tools, and the site will still work.

## Third parties

- **Hosting:** Vercel, as described above.
- **Google Analytics:** provided by Google. Google processes the analytics data described above under its own privacy policy. We receive statistics, not the underlying raw data about individual visitors.
- **Pinterest:** guides include a "Save" button and the footer has a "Follow on Pinterest" link. These are ordinary links. The site loads no Pinterest script, but if you click one, Pinterest's own privacy policy applies. The pages also carry a Pinterest site-verification tag, which does not collect data from readers.
- **Other sites:** guides link to official sources such as the CDC, the EPA and poison-control organizations. Those sites have their own policies.

## If you email us

If you write to the contact address, we receive your email address and your message. We use them to reply and to make corrections. We keep them only as long as needed for that, and we do not sell them or share them for marketing.

## Your choices

To ask what we hold about you, or to ask us to delete it, email the contact address on the [contact page](/contact/). We will reply when possible.

## Children

The site is general information for adults and is not directed to children.

## Changes

If we add advertising, a sign-up form or anything else that collects reader data in a new way, we will update this policy before turning it on, and we will change the effective date above.
